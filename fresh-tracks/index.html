How to Ride

←→Carve / steer. In the air: spin
↑Tuck for speed / skate. In the air: front flip
↓Brake (hockey stop). In the air: back flip
SpaceHold to crouch, let go to ollie. Pop right at the lip to go higher
ASDFGrabs: Indy · Melon · Nose · Tail
QWRGrabs: Mute · Method · Stalefish (hold longer = more points)
ERide the chairlift (at the bottom station). Hold Space to speed up
MTrail map & teleport
CChange camera
TWave to friends
EscPause
Hit a rail or box and you lock on automatically. Hold ← or → as you get on for a boardslide, ↑/↓ to press. In the superpipe you ride wall to wall on your own: just do tricks! Land with your board pointing down the hill or you'll bail.
