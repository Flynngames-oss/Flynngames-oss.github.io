How to play Bobbly Life

Left thumb Touch anywhere on the left and slide to walk (push all the way to run)
Right thumb Drag to look around · pinch with two fingers to zoom
Big buttons They change with what you're doing: Jump, Grab, Slap, Flop on foot · Brake, Horn, Exit in cars · Faster, Slower in planes (stick up/down to climb and dive) · Up, Down in the helicopter
Yellow button Shows up when you can use something: drive a car, open a shop, talk to someone
📱 Phone Spawn cars, fast travel, jobs, gadgets, boss battles · 🗺️ Island map (or tap the minimap)
🔫 Switch blaster · 🎥 First / third person · 😀 Emotes · 💬 Chat (online)
Falling? Tap Parachute · Swimming Swim up / Dive buttons · Fishing Hold Reel in
Best on phones Turn sideways · On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen for full screen

Driving W/S gas & brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake
Helicopter Space up · Shift down
V First-person / third-person camera · M Music on/off
Space while falling Open your parachute 🪂
Rocket Go to the Space Center (east), press E at the rocket. E in space to come home.
Driving Steering is gentler at speed — use Space (handbrake) to slide round corners
E-bikes A/D lean · Shift lean back + W to wheelie · feather W, C (lean forward) and S (rear brake) to hold the balance point · Space hop
Police Riding e-bikes on the street, speeding in town, hitting people, stealing cars or smashing buildings gets you WANTED. Escape far away or get handcuffed and sent to jail!
Secret characters King Flynn (his castle), George (a hidden treehouse in the western woods) and Jacob (top of the Twin Towers) — do their missions to unlock them
Fighter jet Left Click or F fires the cannon
G Switch blaster · Left Click shoot (Right Click still grabs)
Planes W/S throttle · ↑ nose up · ↓ nose down · ← → bank & turn · Airliners at Bobbly International
Diving 🤿 Swim out into Coral Bay (east of town) · C dive · Space swim up · Shift swim fast · watch your air! Scuba gear at the Dive Shop by the pier
Submarine 🟡 W/S thrust · A/D turn · Space rise · Shift/C dive · V for the sonar cockpit (treasure = gold dots)
Treasure 💰 7 sunken chests: in the shipwreck, the underwater cave, the kelp forest and the reef. E to open
Wingsuit 🦅 Jump off something tall, Space while falling · W dive · S flatten out · A/D turn · Space again for the parachute
Train 🚆 Stand on any platform and press E at a carriage to ride, or get in the front cab to drive (W power · S brake · Q horn)
Bobbly Land 🎢 Theme park east of Coral Bay: rollercoaster with a loop, Ferris wheel and bumper cars (train stop: Bobbly Land)
Weather 🌦️ Rain makes roads slippery, storms bring lightning and sometimes a TORNADO. Phone → Weather Machine to change it
Arcade 🕹️ Phone → Arcade: 🗼 Doom Tower, 🪂 Sky Riot, 🦖 Kaiju Smash, or 🎲 Surprise Me for meteors, moon gravity and more. J quits
Party games 🎮 In multiplayer: Phone → Hide & Seek or Cops & Robbers
Boss battles 💥 Phone → Boss Battles: 🤖 a 46 m robot attacks Mega City (shoot its glowing chest core!) or 👽 a UFO invasion. Enter skips the intro
Gadgets 🎒 Phone → Gadgets: 🧑‍🚀 Jetpack (jump, then hold Space) · 🪝 Grappling hook (Q to fire where you look, Q to let go)
Stunt Valley 🔥 Far south-west: drive onto the lift, then fly off the MEGA RAMP over the 64 m gap. Or ride the 💥 Human Cannonball!
☄️ MEGA Rocket Launcher Huge explosions that knock down buildings ($2500 at the Blaster Shop)

Do jobs to earn money 💰: Pizza delivery, Taxi driver, Firefighter, Garbage collector, Lumberjack, Fishing and Races. Spend money on clothes, hats, cars, planes, a helicopter, toy blasters and your very own house! Planes are at the ✈️ airport on the north side, blasters at the 🔫 Blaster Shop in the stunt park. Find all the hidden 🎁 presents around the island — some are far out in the mountains, desert and forest! Buy costumes in the 🦸 Skins tab at the clothing store.
