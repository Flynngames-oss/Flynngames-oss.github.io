How to play Bobbly Life

WASD / Arrows Walk
Shift Run
Space Jump
Mouse Look around (click the game first)
Scroll Zoom camera
Hold Left Click Grab things & people · release to throw
F Slap / chop trees
R Ragdoll (flop over!)
E Interact / get in & out of cars
Tab / P Phone: spawn cars, jobs, map
J Quit current job
T Chat · 1-4 Emotes
Driving W/S gas & brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake
Helicopter Space up · Shift down
V First-person / third-person camera · M Music on/off
Space while falling Open your parachute 🪂
Rocket Go to the Space Center (east), press E at the rocket. E in space to come home.
Driving Steering is gentler at speed — use Space (handbrake) to slide round corners
E-bikes A/D lean · Shift lean back + W to wheelie · feather W, C (lean forward) and S (rear brake) to hold the balance point · Space hop
Police Riding e-bikes on the street, speeding in town, hitting people, stealing cars or smashing buildings gets you WANTED. Escape far away or get handcuffed and sent to jail!
Secret characters King Flynn (his castle), George (a hidden treehouse in the western woods) and Jacob (top of the Twin Towers) — do their missions to unlock them
Fighter jet Left Click or F fires the cannon
G Switch blaster · Left Click shoot (Right Click still grabs)
Planes W/S throttle · ↑ nose up · ↓ nose down · ← → bank & turn · Airliners at Bobbly International

Do jobs to earn money 💰: Pizza delivery, Taxi driver, Firefighter, Garbage collector, Lumberjack, Fishing and Races. Spend money on clothes, hats, cars, planes, a helicopter, toy blasters and your very own house! Planes are at the ✈️ airport on the north side, blasters at the 🔫 Blaster Shop in the stunt park. Find all the hidden 🎁 presents around the island — some are far out in the mountains, desert and forest! Buy costumes in the 🦸 Skins tab at the clothing store.

Multiplayer: click Host Multiplayer and send your friends the link or room code.
