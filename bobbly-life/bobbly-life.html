How to play Bobbly Life

WASD / Arrows Walk
Shift Run
Space Jump
Mouse Look around (click the game first)
Scroll Zoom camera
Hold Left Click Grab things & people · release to throw
F Slap / chop trees
R Ragdoll (flop over!)
E Interact / get in & out of cars
Tab / P Phone: spawn cars, jobs, map
J Quit current job
T Chat · 1-4 Emotes
Driving W/S gas & brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake
Helicopter Space up · Shift down
G Switch blaster · Left Click shoot (Right Click still grabs)
Planes W/S throttle · Space nose up · Shift nose down · A/D turn

Do jobs to earn money 💰: Pizza delivery, Taxi driver, Firefighter, Garbage collector, Lumberjack, Fishing and Races. Spend money on clothes, hats, cars, planes, a helicopter, toy blasters and your very own house! Planes are at the ✈️ airport on the north side, blasters at the 🔫 Blaster Shop in the stunt park. Find the 20 hidden 🎁 presents around the island.
